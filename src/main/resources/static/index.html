chat-ads-eval

Give the service a shopper's message and it picks up to three sponsored products to show beside the assistant's reply. The reply and the ads travel in separate fields, and every ad carries a Sponsored label. Categories on the banned list, such as tobacco, never come back.

This hosted demo runs on a small synthetic catalog with an offline embedder, and nobody has used it for real shopping. It runs on free hosting that sleeps when idle, so the first request after a pause can take about a minute.
